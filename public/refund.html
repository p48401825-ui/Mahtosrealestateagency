Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2025

As a real estate agency, our services are consultation-based. This policy explains when payments are refundable.

1. Service fees

Brokerage and service fees are agreed in writing before any service is provided. Details are confirmed in a service agreement signed by both parties.

2. Cancellation

  • If you cancel a service before work begins, any advance paid is refunded in full, minus administrative charges if any were stated upfront.
  • Once work has started (e.g. property visits, verification, paperwork), fees are generally non-refundable unless otherwise agreed.

3. Refund process

Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 working days to the original payment method. Contact us at info@mahtos.com for refund requests.

4. Property transactions

We do not hold sale proceeds. All payments between buyer and seller are handled directly between the parties or through registered legal channels.

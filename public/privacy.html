Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

Mahto's Real Estate Agency ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact information — name, email, phone number when you register, enquire or contact us.
  • Enquiry details — messages and property preferences you share with us.
  • Usage data — pages visited and basic technical details to improve our website.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide property services.
  • To send service-related communications (not spam marketing without your consent).
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with (a) service providers who help us operate the website, (b) authorities where required by law, and (c) sellers/landlords when you enquire about a specific property and consent is implied by the enquiry.

4. Data security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encryption of passwords and secure connections (HTTPS), to protect your data. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. You can also close your account from your dashboard (contact us to request deletion).

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to maintain your login session and remember preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not work.
